LEAD STORY • INDONESIA • BANTEN

24 killed as fire guts candy factory

The death toll from the blaze at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Cikande, Serang, rose to 24 on Monday, with about 30 workers still missing, as rescuers searched the smouldering ruins and DNA tests began to identify the dead.

300 workers were inside when the fire broke out on Saturday evening
15 people were rescued alive, four of them injured
2–3 weeks to identify victims burned beyond recognition
CLIMATE • ANTALYA

COP31 collapses with no fossil-fuel deal

Two weeks of talks in Turkey ended on Sunday with the “Antalya Pact” rejected: its final text never mentioned coal, oil or gas. The EU and island states refused to endorse it — the longest COP in history, and the first to break down since 2009.

INDIA • NEPAL

A new train links two holy cities

Prime Ministers Narendra Modi and Balendra Shah jointly flagged off the weekly Saryu–Kamala Express between Ayodhya and Janakpur Dham on Monday — a 604 km cross-border line for pilgrims and trade, alongside eight pacts and a major aid package.

MARKETS • NEW YORK

Nvidia brings back Huang; stocks rebound

Jensen Huang returned as Nvidia chief executive 11 days after resigning, and the shares jumped about 33%. The S&P 500 rose 4.82% on Monday, clawing back last week’s AI-driven sell-off as Wall Street rallied.

SPORT • WELLINGTON & LAS VEGAS

Pant’s 128 powers India to a record 720 — and Verstappen wins in Vegas

Rishabh Pant struck 128 off 153 balls as India declared on 720 for nine in Wellington, their highest Test total away from home. In Formula 1, Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole, 0.639 seconds ahead of Lando Norris — his third win on the Strip.
